Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our agriculture harvesting services

How quickly can you provide harvest crews?

We can typically deploy harvest crews within 24-48 hours of your request. For larger operations requiring significant staffing, we recommend providing at least 3-5 days notice to ensure we can assemble the optimal team for your needs.

Do your staff have experience with specific crops like grapes or strawberries?

Yes, we maintain specialized teams with experience in various crops including grapes, strawberries, apples, berries, and other fruits. Each team receives crop-specific training to ensure proper harvesting techniques that preserve quality and maximize yield.

What measures do you take to ensure quality during harvesting?

We implement multiple quality control measures including: initial training on proper harvesting techniques, on-site supervision, regular quality checks, and feedback systems. Our focus is on gentle handling, proper ripeness selection, and minimizing damage to both the crop and plants.

Can you accommodate last-minute changes in staffing needs?

Absolutely. We understand that harvest schedules can change rapidly due to weather conditions and crop maturity. We maintain a flexible pool of workers and can typically adjust crew sizes with 24 hours notice to accommodate your changing needs.

What regions do you serve?

We primarily serve agricultural operations throughout London and the surrounding counties, including Kent, Sussex, Essex, and Hampshire. For larger projects, we can arrange staffing solutions throughout the UK. Contact us to discuss your specific location requirements.
