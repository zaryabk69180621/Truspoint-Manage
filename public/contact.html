Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you start providing services?

We can typically begin services within 3-5 business days after the initial consultation. For emergency security needs, we can deploy personnel within 24 hours.

Do you offer customized service packages?

Yes, we specialize in creating tailored service packages that combine security, shipping, cleaning, and agriculture services based on your specific needs and budget.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Greater London and surrounding areas. Our shipping services extend throughout the UK, with special arrangements for international logistics.

Are your staff properly trained and vetted?

Absolutely. All our security personnel are SIA-licensed and undergo rigorous background checks. Our cleaning, shipping, and agriculture staff receive comprehensive training and are fully insured.

What makes TrusPoint different from other service providers?

Our integrated approach sets us apart. By managing security, shipping, cleaning, and agriculture services through a single provider, we offer streamlined communication, coordinated scheduling, and potential cost savings of 15-25% compared to using multiple providers.
